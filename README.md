# Mayur Enterprises — UPSRTC Bus Washing Contract Accounting & Invoicing

Complete accounting, daily bus log, billing, staff payroll, partner profit settlement, and GST tax invoice system for Mayur Enterprises (Badaun Depot contract).

## Android App (APK)

This repository includes a complete Android WebView application and an automated GitHub Actions workflow to build the APK.

### Download APK
1. Go to the **[Actions tab](https://github.com/ashkichha/mayur-enterprises/actions)**.
2. Click on the latest workflow run: **Build Android APK**.
3. Under **Artifacts** at the bottom, download **Mayur_Enterprises_Badaun_APK.zip**.
4. Extract the zip to get `app-debug.apk` and install it on your Android phone!

## Modules
- **Dashboard**: Revenue, expenses, net partner profit split (Ashish 46.82% / Hariom 53.18%), KPI charts
- **Daily Bus Washing Log**: Daily washed bus count with rate calculation
- **UPSRTC Billing Register**: Monthly taxable amount, 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%), TDS deduction
- **Staff Attendance**: 10-hour shift tracking, relief hours, overtime
- **Staff Payroll & Advances**: ₹10,000 base salary, ₹50/hr overtime, 15th-of-month advance settlement
- **Employee Master**: Add, edit, delete employees with real-time sync across attendance and payroll
- **Partner Expenses**: Cash and online expenses paid by Ashish Singh & Hariom Singh
- **Profit & Loss Settlement**: Office expense deduction (Hariom up to Sep-26, Ashish from Oct-26 onwards), 46.82% / 53.18% partner split
- **Busy Accounting Export**: Excel/CSV export format for Busy Accounting Software
- **Capital & Depreciation Schedule**: Machine & infrastructure investments with WDV depreciation through 2030+
- **GST Tax Invoice**: Print-ready GST compliant tax invoices with Rupee word conversion
